Automation Projects

From manual to almost automated.

Twelve web tools I built to take repeat fleet and logistics work out of spreadsheets, PDFs and Word files. Each one started as a manual task and was redesigned using Lean Six Sigma thinking, so the job became easy.

DefinePick the repeat task that costs the most time or causes the most errors.
MeasureMap the steps: re-keying, copying between files, waiting, checking.
AnalyzeFind the waste and the causes of defects, such as typing errors and mismatched totals.
ImproveBuild a tool that reads the source file, applies the rules and produces the output.
ControlBuilt-in checks, backups and standard formats keep the result consistent every month.
Billing & Cost 01

Toll Fee Dashboard

Before

Autosweep and Easytrip statements of account were retyped into Excel line by line, then checked against the printed totals.

Now

Upload the SOA PDFs and the tool reads every transaction, builds the monthly toll sheet, checks totals against each statement, and charts toll spend by vehicle, tollway and day. Completed sheets come back in to show where toll money went by charging code.

What changed, in practice.

Upload a file, check the result, download the output. The tools run in the browser, so files stay on the user's own computer, and most include backup and restore.

Less re-keying

Data is read straight from PDFs and Excel files instead of being retyped.

Fewer errors

Faster decisions
